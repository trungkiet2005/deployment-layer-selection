# Referee report -- round 2 (self-review of the revised manuscript)

**Recommendation:** Minor revision.

The round-1 concerns are addressed. The indirect-evolutionary-approach
literature is now cited and the differentiation is stated precisely; the
classical invariance lemma and the L-reduction are labelled as recalled facts;
the guard criterion has been generalised to Lemma 12 (mixture-free invasion at
a twin face), which is a statement about arbitrary symmetric matrix games and
is the strongest mathematical contribution in the paper; thresholds are now
normalised by the prize and swept over the two constants of the race; escape
times quantify the valley; a second erosion channel is covered by the restated
hysteresis theorem; and a numerical-methods subsection has been added.

## Remaining points

1. **Numbers checked against the released tables.** Two misquotations were
   found and corrected: the escape-time range in the abstract (was "10^2 to
   10^13", correct value 65 to 3.9e13) and the barrier ratio at B = 10
   (was 4.4, which belongs to p_max = 0.1; at p_max = 0.6 it is 31.7). No
   other quoted scalar disagrees with `results/`.

2. **The valley claim is now correctly hedged** in the abstract, Section 8 and
   the conclusion, and the escape-time table gives the reader the time scale
   directly. No further change needed.

3. **Structured populations remain an unaddressed extension.** The discussion
   now states which mechanisms are expected to survive assortment and which
   are not, with reasons, and flags the question as open. That is the right
   scope for this paper.

4. **Author block and funding are placeholders**, to be completed before
   submission. Highlights, keywords, competing-interest and code-availability
   statements are present.

5. **Presentation.** Four-panel figures were moved to a 2x2 layout so they stay
   legible in a single-column page; legends now sit outside the plotting area
   throughout; the phase portraits are drawn with the EGTtools simplex
   utilities so the neutral AS-CS edge is rendered as the continuum of rest
   points that it is.

## Verdict

The manuscript is internally consistent, every quoted number is regenerated by
the released code, and the claims are matched to the evidence. It is ready for
submission once the author block is completed.
